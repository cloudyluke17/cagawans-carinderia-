The Cagawan Carinderia project is inspiration from my mother's experience when she was running a carinderia. 
It give me an idea that transforming personal experience into a digital platform that enhances access to affordable Filipino ulam.
Rooted in the mission of the National Teachers College the initiative aims to strengthen and support economic resilience and food security.
As a BSIT student that applies technical skills to support traditional Filipino businesses emphasizing user centered design and ensuring.
The project reflects a commitment to social responsibility aiming to connect family heritage with future contributions to society.
